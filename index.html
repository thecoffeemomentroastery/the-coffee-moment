关于烘豆

The Roastery

我们位于柔佛古来（Kulai, Johor），专注于精品咖啡。店内所有的咖啡豆均由我们自家进行小批量烘焙，以确保风味的干净与稳定。

Located in Kulai, Johor, we focus on specialty coffee. All beans are roasted in-house in small batches to ensure clarity and consistency.

烘焙理念

Our Philosophy

我们追求平衡且具辨识度的风味。通过对每一批生豆的精准控制，呈现出不同产区应有的地域特性。

We seek balanced and distinct flavors. Through precise control of every batch, we highlight the unique terroir of each origin.
